# The correct answer that was premature in January

**Rubén Abella · SPE Audit**

On 1 January 2022, a NASA spacecraft was on its way to Dimorphos. Consider two answers about DART’s outcome as known on that date. “The spacecraft is scheduled to hit Dimorphos” describes a published plan. “DART hit Dimorphos in 2022” describes an accomplished event. The second sentence became true on 26 September. Changing the verb changes what an auditor should conclude. [NASA launch announcement](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-spacex-launch-dart-first-test-mission-to-defend-planet-earth/) · [NASA impact account](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/darts-final-images-prior-to-impact/)

The second sentence comes from `dart-09`, a case I wrote to study this problem. No provider generated this example for the experiment. The original SPE detector compares the year in the answer with the cutoff year. Both are 2022, so it issues no alert. A reader with the date of impact can decide the historical question without guessing how an answer with those words might have been produced. ## The number a product owner needs

The dated matcher in edition 1.0 receives the target event, its date and a small set of expressions for recognizing what the speaker commits to. In 96 constructed cases, 32 presented a later outcome as complete. The matcher detected 24 of the 32 positives (75% sensitivity) and made an automatic decision in 84 of the 96 cases (87.5% coverage). It referred eight positives and four controls for review. All 24 alerts matched the study labels. Both the rules and the sentences were developed around the same four missions. [Results and decision counts](https://github.com/joinsnipe/SPE_AUDIT/blob/main/research/temporal-audit/study/results/summary.json)

Changing the wording changed the cost of operating the method. On 16 sentences written in a separate phase, the same matcher detected **0 of 8 positives** and left **14 of 16 cases for review**. Its two decisions were controls. A product owner budgeting for reviewers based only on the first twelve referrals would be transferring a rate between two very different collections of sentences. The challenge shares events with development and has no independent scientific adjudication. Estimating production workload requires sampling the service’s own answers and measuring review time as well as frequency. [Challenge sentences](https://github.com/joinsnipe/SPE_AUDIT/blob/main/research/temporal-audit/study/data/challenge.json)

## Where did the information come from?

The supplement asks a different question using a fictional identifier called a canary. Across 40 matched cases, each with its own code, I supplied the document containing that code, withheld it or replaced it with a document containing a different code. I repeated the three conditions in three local programs. That gives 360 calls with 40 matched units per contrast. The inputs and outputs are available for inspection.

The reader program returned the target code 40/40 times with the original document, 0/40 without it and 0/40 with the replacement. In the last condition it returned the replacement code 40/40 times. A generic program never returned the target. A third program had a separate input containing the target and returned it 40/40 times in **each** condition. If all we retain is one answer with the original document present, the reader and the third program look equally convincing. Changing the document and watching what persists reveals the difference. The public codes are predictable because the reproduction seed is published. These findings describe the deterministic programs in this experiment. Attribution to a training example, a retrieved document or a provider in a historical case requires further observations. [Design and results of the 40 triplets](https://github.com/joinsnipe/SPE_AUDIT/tree/main/research/temporal-audit/v11/interventions)

## A case file the other party can read

The local adapter records the serialized request, cutoff, selection policy and returned text. Someone with a separately retained digest can check whether the bytes still match. Contesting the content also requires the claim, versioned sources, their dates and an explicit rule for distinguishing forecasts from completed assertions. Establishing what happened inside a remote service requires evidence from that service. Local capture alone leaves the question open. The public case file allows a reader to see where each conclusion ends. [Full English paper](https://github.com/joinsnipe/SPE_AUDIT/blob/main/research/temporal-audit/PAPER_EN.pdf) · [Capture implementation](https://github.com/joinsnipe/SPE_AUDIT/blob/main/research/temporal-audit/study/capture.py)

A technical engagement can start with one dated question and an agreed set of cases. The organization and auditor specify where capture takes place, what sources may be retained and how a reviewer will handle disagreements. Deliverables can include a trial integration, reproducible case files and a report separating alerts, pending reviews and attribution limits. The repository allows internal evaluation and testing under its Business Source License 1.1 and requires a separate commercial license for the commercial uses it describes. An integration or technical audit should define its scope and usage rights. Its deliverable is evidence open to challenge. A regulatory conformity determination or an accredited assessment would require an applicable procedure and competent authority.
